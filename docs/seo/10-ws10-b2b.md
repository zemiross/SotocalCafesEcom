## 3. Connecting a CRM (your choice, not made)

The form is Shopify's contact form: each submission arrives as an email with labelled lines (Nome, Estabelecimento, Tipo, Distrito, Localidade, Telefone, Consumo mensal, Melhor hora, Origem). Three ways to feed a CRM, simplest first:

| Option | How | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Mailbox forwarding | Send the form to the sales mailbox (Q20) and forward that mailbox to your CRM's inbound-email address | No new code or app; fields stay as text lines |
| 2. Automation tool | A Zapier, Make or similar flow reading that mailbox and creating a lead | Structured fields; another account and monthly cost |
| 3. Direct form post | Replace the contact form with a post to the CRM's own form endpoint | Best data, but the lead leaves Shopify, so the consent text and privacy policy must name that processor |

**Decided 17/11/2026 (D101, replacing D86): Resend.** The form answers are emailed to someone at Sotocal with a copy to José; there is no CRM. Code, tests and checklist: `store-setup/lead-endpoint/`. Not deployed; it needs a verified sending domain, a rate limit and a privacy-policy line. I have not connected any CRM, and I have not written lead data into any external account. Whichever CRM is chosen, the privacy policy must name it.
